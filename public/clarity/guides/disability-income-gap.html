How do I estimate my disability income gap?

A disability income gap is essential spending minus income and disability benefits expected to continue if you are unable to work.

The calculation or starting point

Illustrative gap = max(0, essential monthly expenses − continuing income − expected monthly disability benefit).

Worked example or review questions

Essential spending of $5,000, other income of $2,000 and a stated disability benefit of $1,000 leave a $2,000 monthly gap. A $15,000 reserve covers 7.5 months under constant assumptions.

What to consider

Review definition of disability, elimination period, benefit period, limits, exclusions, offsets and taxation. The estimate does not determine eligibility or benefits.
