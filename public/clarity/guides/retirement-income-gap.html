How to calculate your retirement income gap

Your monthly retirement income gap is planned spending minus dependable monthly income, including Social Security and pensions. Treat any positive difference as a gap to explore.

The calculation or starting point

Monthly gap = max(0, monthly spending − Social Security − other regular monthly income). Annual gap = monthly gap × 12.

Worked example or review questions

A household spends $5,500 per month and expects $2,800 from Social Security and $700 from a pension. The gap is $2,000 monthly, or $24,000 annually. A $300,000 reserve divided by that annual gap is 12.5 years in a deliberately simplified illustration, not a sustainable withdrawal estimate.

What to consider

Consider timing, taxes, inflation, irregular expenses, longevity and investment fluctuations. An annuity is not automatically the right way to cover a gap.
