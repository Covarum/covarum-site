What should I review in my existing life insurance policy?

Review your existing policy before considering replacement: insured person, death benefit, premium obligations, beneficiary designations and the responsibilities you want covered.

The calculation or starting point

Gather the policy contract, latest statement, premium schedule, endorsements and any in-force illustration. Do not upload sensitive documents here.

Worked example or review questions

For term: check level-premium period and conversion deadline. For cash value: check guarantees, premium obligations, surrender charges and outstanding loans.

What to consider

A replacement may involve new underwriting, charges, exclusions or contestability periods. Do not cancel existing coverage until replacement is in force and you understand the consequences.
